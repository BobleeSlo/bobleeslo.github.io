# MileLog Support

Email **[support@milelog.net](mailto:support@milelog.net)**. We usually reply within two working days, in English or Slovenian.

To help us answer faster, include your iPhone model, the iOS version and the MileLog version from **Settings → About**. For a trip that was not recorded, attach the **Detection log** (Settings → Auto-detect trips → Detection log).

## Common questions

**Trips are not recorded automatically.**
Open **Settings → Auto-detect trips** and check that automatic detection is on and that location access for MileLog is set to **Always** (iOS Settings → MileLog → Location). On the Free plan, automatic recording pauses when the monthly allowance is used up; manual recording keeps working.

**The wrong car is used for a trip.**
Pair each vehicle with its car Bluetooth audio device in the **Vehicles** tab. You can change the vehicle of any trip in the trip details.

**How do I generate the monthly report or potni nalog?**
**Settings → Own-car monthly report** or the company-car report. You can review and uncheck individual trips before the PDF is created. Reports require MileLog Paid.

**How do I restore or cancel my subscription?**
Restore: **Settings → Plan → Restore purchases**. Cancel: iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → MileLog**. Deleting the app or your MileLog account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

**How do I delete my account?**
**Settings → Account → Delete account** permanently deletes your account and all cloud data (trips, vehicles, receipts, settings).
